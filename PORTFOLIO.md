# RAKENNE. — Construction website concept

**Category:** Construction / small business.  
**Description:** A fictional Finnish construction-company concept built as an independent, editorial web experience. The design balances an industrial palette, expressive typography, and clear service-led conversion paths.

**Design goals:** Build trust, make services easy to scan, and present a confident technical character.  
**Technologies:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, CSS.  
**Key features:** Responsive navigation, accessible validated demo contact form, reduced-motion support, noindex metadata.  
**Project card:** Bold construction-company concept with a dark industrial identity.  
**Suggested screenshots:** Hero desktop; service cards; mobile navigation; contact section.
